On 26 August 1914 the [Battle of Komarów](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitwa_pod_Komarowem_(1914)) began in the Zamość region. The Austro-Hungarian 4th Army of General [Moritz von Auffenberg](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moritz_Auffenberg) clashed with the Russian 5th Army of General [Pavel Plehve](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paweł_Plehwe) on the territory of the Kingdom of Poland, between [Zamość](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamość), Komarów and [Tomaszów Lubelski](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomaszów_Lubelski).

## Two armies on the march

After the victory [at Kraśnik](/koniec-bitwy-pod-krasnikiem), the Austro-Hungarian Chief of Staff, Franz Conrad von Hötzendorf, ordered the 4th Army to advance north towards Chełm. At the same time the Russian 5th Army was marching south from the opposite direction, from Chełm and Hrubieszów. Neither side knew exactly where the enemy's main forces were. Auffenberg, reinforced by the Tyrolean corps of Archduke Joseph Ferdinand, decided to outflank the Russians on both sides.

## The first day

The first blow fell on the Russian XXV Corps near Zamość. The Austro-Hungarian II Corps from Vienna inflicted heavy losses on it and forced it to retreat north towards Krasnystaw. Further Russian corps, however, were arriving from the east, and in the following days the fighting spread along the whole front from Zamość to Tomaszów, and at times the Austrians' position became dangerous.

## What the following days brought

The battle lasted until 2 September. The Austro-Hungarians almost encircled Plehve's army, but at the last moment the Russians slipped away behind the Bug, losing many soldiers and prisoners. Auffenberg won a victory for which he later received the title of baron with the honorific "von Komarów". It did not, however, change the fate of the whole campaign, because in eastern Galicia the Russians were at the same time pushing the weaker Austro-Hungarian armies back towards Lemberg.

The fighting took place on Polish soil, and Poles served in both armies. Six years later, in 1920, another [Battle of Komarów](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitwa_pod_Komarowem) was fought in the same area, this time between Polish cavalry and Budyonny's Cavalry Army.
