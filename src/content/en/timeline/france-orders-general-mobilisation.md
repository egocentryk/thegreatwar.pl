On the afternoon of 1 August 1914 the government of [France](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/III_Republika_Francuska) ordered general [mobilisation](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobilizacja). Thousands of telegrams were sent to military commanders, prefects and mayors. Notices were posted on the walls of towns, church bells rang, and in the villages the news was announced by gendarmes. The first day of mobilisation was 2 August.

The decision came after several days of mounting tension. The day before, Germany had demanded that France declare whether it would remain neutral in a German-Russian war. The prime minister, [René Viviani](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/René_Viviani), replied only that France would act in accordance with its interests. The Chief of the General Staff, [Joseph Joffre](/postacie/joseph-joffre), had long been warning that every day of delay gave an advantage to Germany, which had already begun its war preparations.

At the same time France tried to show that it was not the aggressor. As early as 30 July the government had ordered troops to be withdrawn 10 kilometres from the German border to avoid accidental incidents. The government proclamation announcing mobilisation, signed among others by President [Raymond Poincaré](/postacie/raymond-poincare), assured citizens that mobilisation did not yet mean war.

The public mood was grave but resolute. The previous evening the leader of the French Socialists, [Jean Jaurès](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Jaurès), the best-known opponent of war, had been shot dead in Paris. Even so, the Socialists and trade unionists did not call for resistance to mobilisation. A few days later all the main political forces concluded a "sacred union" (union sacrée) in defence of the country.

Mobilisation lasted from 2 to 18 August. In that time more than three million men had to be called up, uniformed, armed and transported by rail. On 3 August Germany declared war on France, and the [French Army](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armée_de_terre) under Joffre's command began carrying out its plan for an offensive against Germany.
